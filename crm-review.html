For context, iGaming email benchmarks typically sit around 20–25% opens and 2–3% clicks. Casino007's onboarding journey is roughly double that on opens and triple on clicks, and the promotional calendar is comfortably at or above the benchmark too. The list is also clean: deliverability is 97.8%, and complaints are almost non-existent.

✅ What's working

The welcome email is the star

51.9% of new players open it and 13.4% click — the single best-performing message in the account. Whatever is being done with that subject line and offer, it is landing.

Arabic-first was the right call

99% of delivered mail is Arabic, and it carries the engagement. The English templates exist and are ready, so the programme can scale into new markets without a rebuild.

Cashback is the strongest promo theme

The Monday Cashback and Cashback 2 sends opened at 32.9% and 33.4% — the best of any campaign series. A recurring, predictable reward clearly resonates.

Smaller, targeted sends click harder

Promo Batch 2 Part 2 clicked at 10.0% and Batch 3 at 6.5%, versus roughly 3% on the largest blasts. Tighter targeting is already proving itself.

The list is healthy

97.8% deliverability, bounces at 2% or less on nearly every send, zero unsubscribes on most campaigns and six spam reports in total. That is a well-maintained database.

4,529 messages sent automatically

Four live journeys reach players at sign-up, at Day 10, after a first deposit and after a second — reaching players at the right moment with no manual effort.

The CRM side of push is working as designed: messages are built in both languages, queued correctly and dispatched on schedule. What determines how many actually arrive is something that lives on the website rather than in the CRM — web push can only reach a browser that holds an active subscription, and a subscription is only created when a player accepts the opt-in prompt on site.

That makes push the biggest piece of free reach available to Casino007 right now. Every player who accepts the prompt becomes contactable at zero marginal cost for as long as they keep the browser. Growing that pool is a small change on the site, and the CRM is already built to take advantage of it the day it happens.

🚀 Where we grow next

Eight suggestions, ordered by the size of the prize relative to the effort involved. Each one builds on something that is already working.

1. Switch on a win-back journey

Highest impact

1,269 players sit in the soft-churn, churn, dormant and declining segments with no automated message reaching them. They are the single largest reachable group in the account, and they already know the brand.

Build one journey off Inactive 1 (590 players) first: a 'we miss you' message with a small no-strings bonus, then a second nudge three days later for non-openers. Reuse the onboarding template that already opens at 51.9%. If it converts even 2%, that is a dozen players back per week.

2. Grow the push subscriber base

Highest free upside

Push messages are queuing and sending correctly, but very few browsers hold a live subscription, so the audience reached is a fraction of the email list.

Review where and when the opt-in prompt appears on site — prompting immediately on landing converts poorly. Trigger it instead after a positive moment: a completed registration, a first deposit or a win. Add a short line explaining what players get. Every accepted prompt is permanent, free reach.

3. Give the welcome email a bigger call to action

Quick win

Message one already pulls 51.9% opens and 13.4% clicks — the highest intent moment in the entire player lifecycle.

Test a single large deposit button above the fold against the current layout, and test one subject line variant per week. A one-point lift on the welcome email is worth more than a whole extra campaign.

4. Keep sends small and targeted

Quick win

The tightest sends are clicking at 6–10%, while the largest blasts sit nearer 3%. The pattern is consistent across every batch.

Default to segment-based sends rather than whole-list blasts. The segments already exist — Active bettors (120), Low risk/loyal (80) and High risk (62) each deserve their own message and offer rather than a shared one.

5. Make cashback a protected weekly fixture

Build on a winner

Cashback campaigns open at 21.8–33.4%, the strongest theme in the calendar, and it gives players a reason to return on a predictable day.

Lock Monday cashback into the calendar as a recurring flow rather than a manual campaign, so it goes out reliably and the team's time goes to new ideas instead.

6. Add a VIP and loyalty track

Medium term

200 players sit in Active bettors and Low risk (loyal) with no dedicated journey. These are the most valuable players in the database.

A simple tiered track — a thank-you message, a monthly reload matched to their level and early access to Drops & Wins — protects revenue that is already there. Retention is cheaper than acquisition.

7. Introduce a holdout group and subject-line tests

Measurement

Everything currently goes to everyone, so it is not yet possible to prove how much revenue the CRM programme itself is generating.

Hold back a random 10% from each flow and compare deposit rates after 30 days. It costs almost nothing and turns the programme's results into a number the business can bank.

📌 In short

The foundations are in good shape. Deliverability is high, the list is clean, four journeys run without supervision and the onboarding sequence performs at roughly double the industry benchmark. The next phase is less about fixing and more about extending: reaching the players who have gone quiet, growing the push audience, and putting a measurement framework around it so the programme's contribution is visible in the numbers.
